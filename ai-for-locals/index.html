What I’m Trying To Do

AI is not just for big companies. Used sensibly, it can help smaller businesses reduce manual work, improve communication, understand their data, respond faster, create better documentation and operate with more confidence.

My aim is to help local businesses take part in the AI boom without being overwhelmed by it. This is not about selling you a big “AI transformation” project. It is about sitting down, understanding how your business actually runs, and looking for practical ways to save time, reduce manual admin, improve visibility or make day-to-day work easier.

If I cannot see a clear way for AI, automation or better reporting to help, I will tell you honestly rather than trying to sell you something you do not need.

Good first questions

These are the kinds of questions we can work through together. Here’s how I’d answer them.

What is AI actually useful for in my business?

Used sensibly, AI can help draft and tidy up writing, answer common enquiries, summarise documents, pull useful information out of spreadsheets and records, speed up admin and give you clearer reporting. It works best on repetitive, time-consuming tasks — not as a magic replacement for how your business runs.

What tools should I be looking at?

For most small businesses, general assistants like ChatGPT and Claude cover a lot of ground. The right choice depends on what you actually need. I can help you pick something suitable and avoid paying for tools you won’t use.

Are my staff using AI safely?

Worth checking. The main risks are putting sensitive customer, financial or health information into tools without understanding where it goes, and trusting answers without review. I can help you set up simple, plain-English guidelines so your team gets the benefit without the risk.

Could I automate some of this admin?

Often, yes. Enquiries, onboarding, follow-ups, scheduling, quotes and document generation are common candidates. I look at how your work actually flows and suggest lightweight automations that save time without adding complexity.

Can AI help with marketing, hiring, reporting or documentation?

Yes — for clearer website copy, listings and emails; for drafting job ads and screening notes; for turning raw numbers into readable reports; and for building how-to guides and standard procedures. The aim is content that sounds like you, not generic AI output.

Is there anything I should avoid?

Avoid big, expensive “AI transformation” projects before you understand the value, sharing sensitive data carelessly, and trusting AI output without a human check. Start small, prove it helps, and only build what earns its place.

What would be a sensible first step?

A free, no-pressure AI review. We sit down, I listen to what’s taking up your time, and I suggest one or two practical places AI, automation or better reporting might help. If I can’t see a clear benefit, I’ll tell you honestly.
