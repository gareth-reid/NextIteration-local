What I'm Trying To Do

AI is not just for big tech companies. Used sensibly, it can help smaller businesses, and even individuals, reduce manual work, improve communication, understand their data, respond faster, create better documentation and operate with more confidence & efficiency.

My aim is to help local businesses take part in the AI boom without being overwhelmed by it.

It is about sitting down, understanding how your business actually runs, and looking for practical ways to save time, reduce manual admin, improve data visibility, analyse operations or make day-to-day work easier.

  • Avoid wasting money on the wrong tools
  • Reduce repetitive admin and manual work

  • Train staff to use AI safely and sensibly
